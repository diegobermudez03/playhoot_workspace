Direction C — Soft Clay

Thesis: a toy box. Claymorphism — puffy extruded surfaces with soft outer shadows and inner highlights, big radii, a candy palette on a cool cloud background, chubby rounded type (Baloo 2 + Quicksand). The most playful and most distinctive silhouette of the three; buttons beg to be pressed. Risks: the heavy surface treatment needs discipline to stay AA-contrast and to not tire adults on dense screens (teacher dashboards, admin); it is also the strongest commitment — every component carries the clay look.
