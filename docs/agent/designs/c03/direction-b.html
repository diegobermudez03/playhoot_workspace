Direction B — Warm Paper

Thesis: crafted stationery. Cream canvas, layered white paper cards with soft, real shadows, terracotta and deep teal accents, a soft serif (Fraunces) for display with Nunito Sans body. It feels like a well-made classroom kit: warm, calm and adult — the direction that most says “safe to pay” to a teacher. Liveliness comes from the mascot and gentle motion rather than loud chrome. Risk: closest of the three to conventional ed-tech, so identity leans on the serif voice, the palette and the cat.
