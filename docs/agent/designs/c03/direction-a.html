Direction A — Ink & Sticker

Thesis: a tabletop-game box brought to the screen. Warm paper, honest near-black ink, flat accent colors, visible 2 px outlines and hard offset shadows. Buttons physically press down, cards tilt like stickers, the mascot is a flat vector cat with Lottie-style blinks and tail swings. It is deliberately drawn — the opposite of gradient-and-glass AI defaults — and the ink-on-paper base makes WCAG AA contrast the default, not a retrofit. Display type Fredoka, body Nunito (both OFL, self-hosted later).
