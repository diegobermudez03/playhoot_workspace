# T-108 — H1 walkthrough and verification

**Tier:** B (Opus · medium) · **Size:** M · **Repos:** backend, workspace · **Milestone:** H1 · **Requires:** T-101…T-107

## Goal
Close H1 the way P16§2 demands: demonstrable behaviors with failures and retries, each check linked to its rule; move what passed to `verified`.

## Read first
- `docs/agent/tasks/T-101.md` … `T-107.md` Results; SPEC_MAP §16, §17, §22; P32§10 (the launch demonstrations — do locally what is demonstrable without a deployment).

## Deliverables
1. An executable walkthrough (Go test or script under the backend repo, runnable with the local database) demonstrating at least:
   - a free spend spike without revenue → restriction → selective pause → automatic shutdown; queues and retries stop; restart does not lift it; manual step-up reopening, gradual;
   - concurrent admissions near a cap not overspending it;
   - Resend quota exhaustion → controlled switch to SES; accepted-by-one never resent; suppression honored;
   - a failing queue task → backoff → dead → admin retry succeeds; cancel works;
   - notification accepted once under retries; email failure leaves the in-app entry and never shows delivered; read semantics;
   - declaration recorded once, divergent answer refused, blocked market refused with a service-restriction reason;
   - analytics events arriving idempotently from real game/session writes.
2. Site data of everything demonstrated moved to `verified`; validator at 0.
3. `GAPS.md` reviewed for H1: record what intentionally remains (e.g. existing flows not yet consulting compliance/plan/budget — their milestones reconcile).
4. `PLAN.md`: H1 closed; confirm or adjust the H2 note; `STATE.md` points at the next milestone's design task (T-200 unless the plan says otherwise).

## Acceptance criteria
- Repo checks green including the walkthrough; every demonstrated behavior cites its rule (patch§) in the test names or comments-free assertions table.

## Out of scope
Deployment-level cost controls and provider-real demonstrations (delivery 2 / launch; they stay in `CREATOR_TODO.md`).
