### 2 · What the verification found and fixed (D-044)

1. **Joining asked for a declaration, against C-01.** The join admission ran the full market gate,
   so anyone without a declaration was answered `declaration_required` wherever the place had an
   age — every open market — and locally always. The admission now uses `marketgate.Gate.Admit`:
   nothing is asked, the person counts as meeting the age unless their minority is known, and only
   a restriction refuses. Shown in the walkthrough and live: a guest joining an unknown Session on
   the running backend now gets `invalid_session`, not `declaration_required`. The frontend dropped
   the declare-to-join outcome.
2. **The join screen showed `market_restricted` as an unexpected error** — its switch had no case.
   It has its own calm outcome.
3. **Dialogs opened on their close button.** React never writes `autofocus` to the page, so
   `showModal()` focused the ×, and the deletion dialog never opened on Cancel as designed.
   `Dialog` now focuses an element marked `data-autofocus`; the gallery shows it.
4. `robots.txt` did not disallow H3's private pages; it does now, and `verify:seo` checks it.
5. Two H1 walkthrough steps waited a fixed 2 s for retries to run out; on a slow local database the
   full suite failed them intermittently. They wait for their condition now (up to 30 s).
